## Beyond Code

I enjoy literature, photography, and learning Japanese. Fiction draws me toward the inner lives of other people; photography makes me pay attention to light, space, and details I might otherwise walk past.

I want to make everyday responsibilities easier to manage, so there is more room for curiosity, creative work, and unplanned afternoons. That is what connects many of my projects: a place to learn, a way to keep memories, a world to spend time in, or a small invitation to try something new.

### A Few Things I Care About

- **Tools that leave room for life.** A useful system should reduce the effort of keeping things in order and give me more freedom over my time.
- **Clarity with depth.** I like simple interfaces and clear explanations that preserve the details needed to understand something.
- **Quiet, personal spaces.** Soft light, thoughtful colors, and a few well-chosen objects can make a place feel like my own. I bring the same care to digital spaces.
- **Curiosity without a checklist.** Some interests can grow into long-term projects. Others can simply be an afternoon spent reading, wandering, or making something by hand.

I'm still exploring how these interests fit together. These projects are part of that process.
